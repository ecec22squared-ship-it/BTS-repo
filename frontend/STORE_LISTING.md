## Full description (~3500 chars — for both stores)

```
🌌 YOUR STORY. THEIR LEGEND.

Beyond the Stars is an AI-narrated sci-fi role-playing game where every traveler writes their own galaxy — and every galaxy remembers. A real-time AI Game Master narrates your personal saga scene by scene, while a living community archive tracks the choices, victories, and ruins left behind by every other player who has ever rolled the dice.

No two stories are alike. No pre-written path. Just you, your hero, and a galaxy that quietly takes notes.

🎭 AN AI GAME MASTER WHO KNOWS YOU
Powered by a state-of-the-art narrative AI, the Game Master adapts to your character, your tone, and the consequences you've already lived through. Every scene is generated for you — cinematic, reactive, and never repeated.

👤 PERSISTENT PLAYER PROFILE
Your profile travels with you across every campaign:
  • Heroes you've forged
  • Eras you've conquered
  • Triumphs, despairs, and the friends you made (or burned) along the way
  • Notable scenes pinned to your personal log
Retire a character and they don't vanish — they become part of your legacy.

🪞 CROSS PATHS WITH YOUR OLD SELF — OR OTHERS
This is the part no other text RPG does. Retired characters from your profile (and from every other traveler in the galaxy) can resurface as NPCs in your live story. Walk into a cantina and meet the smuggler you played three campaigns ago — now a wanted legend. Stumble onto the ruins of someone else's last firefight. The galaxy is a shared memory.

🎲 NARRATIVE DICE WITH CONSEQUENCE
Every action resolves through an authentic narrative dice pool. Successes, advantages, triumphs, threats, and despair don't just decide pass/fail — they directly shape the next sentence of your story.

🧬 FORGE YOUR HERO
A five-stage genetics facility lets you build a hero from the ground up:
  • 10+ species, each with unique abilities and starting skills
  • Careers from smugglers and bounty hunters to resonance-sensitive mystics and tech aces
  • Specializations that shape how the galaxy reacts to you
  • Write your own backstory — the AI weaves it into the plot

🪐 IMMERSIVE ENVIRONMENT ENGINE
  • Procedurally generated ambient audio adapts to every planet, cantina, and firefight
  • First-person viewport with dynamic lighting per environment
  • Holo-tables, bio-labs, and comm chambers that feel lived-in
  • Character portraits and scene illustrations generated on demand

🗓️ ERAS THAT REWRITE THE GALAXY
Seven distinct eras — from the Replicant Wars and Vex Directive 66 to the rise of the Neo-Concordat, the Vrakxul Revenge, and the Vorthak frontier. Each era rewrites the factions, NPCs, and politics around you.

🌐 COMMUNITY GAME HISTORY
Global Events track major decisions across every traveler in the galaxy. Chase the same bounty as another player, liberate the same colony, or stumble onto the aftermath of someone else's firefight. Your stories overlap, intersect, and echo.

💰 FAIR & FLEXIBLE
New travelers begin with 500 free credits. Every action costs just 1 coin. Top up occasionally via the Galactic Banking Clan, or subscribe to unlock entire eras and bonus coins each month. No ads, ever.

📱 BUILT FOR YOUR PHONE
  • Thumb-friendly one-handed UI
  • Swipe-right for the Holo-Comm Chamber
  • Continue exactly where you left off
  • Export and share your character cards and scenes

🎧 SOUND ON, HEADPHONES UP
Engine hums, cantina chatter, blaster wail, resonance-winds — the procedural audio layer reacts live to every scene you enter.
```
